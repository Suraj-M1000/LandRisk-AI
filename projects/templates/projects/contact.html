PROJECT INFORMATION

About This Initiative

LandRisk AI is an independent educational project inspired by SIH26017: Predictive Analytics for Early Detection of Land Acquisition Delays. It explores full-stack development, data processing, and machine learning integration.

Its current model uses synthetic training data and is not validated for real-world forecasting.
